BLACK WIDOW IMAGE COMPARISON — NOT USED BY THE GAME

The game uses widow-infiltration-walk.png and widow-relay-stance.png from the parent assets folder. These candidates were downloaded and inspected for fit. The checkerboard-backed previews and combat poses were rejected because they looked like a fight scene or did not composite cleanly over the vault.
