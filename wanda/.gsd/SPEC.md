# Project Specification: Wanda – The Portal Awakening

**Status:** FINALIZED (Planning Lock)

## 1. Project Overview
An interactive, cinematic 2.5D browser game featuring Wanda Maximoff (Scarlet Witch), inspired by her telekinetic and Chaos Magic abilities in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

## 2. Technology Stack & Constraints
- **Allowed:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).
- **Prohibited:** React, Vue, Angular, Bootstrap, Tailwind, Three.js, Phaser, Unity, and all third-party external libraries/frameworks.
- **Audio:** Web Audio API (procedural synthesis without external audio files).

## 3. Core Mechanics & Sequence
1. **Phase 1: Wanda's Meditation**
   - Wanda in floating meditation pose at screen center (legs crossed, hands relaxed, eyes closed).
   - Dark 2.5D mystical atmosphere with floating particles, glowing rune floor, red atmospheric lighting.
   - 3 floating objects around her (Ancient Crystal, Mystical Energy Ring, Mysterious Artifact).
2. **Phase 2: Crystal Alignment Challenge**
   - 3 concentric rotating rings around the crystal.
   - Rotated with Arrow Keys (Left/Right) or touch/click buttons.
   - Rings lock when aligned with target zenith marker.
   - Once all 3 rings align, crystal illuminates and moves to portal node 1.
3. **Phase 3: Energy Stabilization Challenge**
   - Mystical Energy Ring oscillates erratically.
   - Player holds Spacebar (or button) to channel Chaos energy.
   - Releasing inside the harmonic green zone stabilizes 1/3 anchors.
   - 3 successful stabilizations lock the ring into portal node 2.
4. **Phase 4: Debugging Interruption (Anomaly)**
   - Third artifact begins moving, then freezes mid-air.
   - Screen glitch & distortion effect + glitch audio.
   - "CHAOS MAGIC SYSTEM" error dialog appears with error code 003.
   - Reserved placeholder area for browser DevTools debugging challenge.
   - "BYPASS FOR TESTING" button resolves the anomaly and places artifact into portal node 3.
5. **Phase 5: Portal Construction**
   - 3 objects in equilateral triangle around the vortex.
   - Chaos lightning arcs connect Wanda's hands to objects and objects to each other.
   - Portal expands with rotating runic rings, accretion vortex particles, and cosmic singularity.
6. **Phase 6: Entering the Portal**
   - Wanda descends from floating meditation to standing pose.
   - Scarlet glowing eyes open.
   - Camera tilts in 2.5D perspective.
   - Wanda turns, walks into the portal, and dissolves into magical energy.
   - Portal implodes, screen fades to black, cinematic victory card appears.
