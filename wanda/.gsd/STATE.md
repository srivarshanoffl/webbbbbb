5. `js/wanda.js`: Character state controller (levitation, aura flare, standing morph, turning, walking into portal).
6. `js/game.js`: 6-phase finite state machine, keyboard controls, bypass handler, and cinematic sequence runner.
7. `.gsd/SPEC.md` & `.gsd/ROADMAP.md`: Project specifications and phase roadmap.
